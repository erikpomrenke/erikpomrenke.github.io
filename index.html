Writing

With an academic background and years of experience in journalism, I have written on a variety of different topics across several platforms.


  • Telos Press: As an editorial intern, I contributed several pieces to Telos Press, an academic publication for political theory, literary criticism, and intellectual history.

  • MA Thesis: I wrote this for my MA in Medieval Literature and History at the University of Iceland. I was interested in questions of potential class consciousness and ideology in the Icelandic sagas, and how these were discussed in the context of literary representations of work. Available via Skemman or download.

  • Reykjavik Grapevine: The Reykjavik Grapevine is a popular street magazine in Iceland. Focused on travel and tourism, I wrote music reviews, conducted interviews, and contributed general culture pieces. Check out my work.

  • Iceland Review: Iceland Review was previously a prestigious English-language publication in Iceland, with a focus on travel writing and tourism. In addition to writing daily news for the website, I also contributed editorial articles to the print magazine, with a focus on society, politics, language, culture, and nature conservation. Read my work.

  • Icelandair: In my current role as Technical SEO Specialist, I also contribute to the Icelandair Content Hub. Some recent pieces of mine include The Icelandic Language and a Guide to the Mountains of Iceland.
